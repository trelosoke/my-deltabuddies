# Contribution Guide

**🚧 Warning: The project is under active development and it's not open for contribution at the moment.**

We're currently focused on delivering the MVP (Minimum Viable Product). External pull requests will only be reviewed and accepted after the MVP launch, when we open the project for contributions.

In the meanwhile, feel free to:

- Open **Issues** for bug reporting and suggest ideas.
- **Fork** the project to experiment and develop your own ideas.

We appreciate your interest and ask for your patience until the MVP release.
